# Design System: Simple Alarm Mobile App
## 1. Style Summary
Minimalist mobile alarm app with high legibility for tabular numerals and a calm visual hierarchy. Combines crisp white surfaces (`#FFFFFF`) and soft ice-blue cards (`#EEF2F8`) with deep royal navy (`#0B336B`) primary blocks for the next alarm hero card and active controls.
## 2. Colors
- primary: #0B336B
- primary-foreground: #FFFFFF
- secondary: #EEF2F8
- accent: #16498E
- background: #F4F5F7
- surface: #FFFFFF
- border: #D5DCE6
- text: #111827
- text-muted: #6B7280
- danger: #DC2626
## 3. Typography
- Font: Plus Jakarta Sans (Google Fonts) with `tabular-nums` for all alarm times and countdowns.
- display: 36px Bold, h1: 28px Bold, h2: 16px SemiBold, body: 14px Regular/Medium, label: 12px Medium, caption: 11px Regular.
## 4. Spacing & Radius
- sm: 8px, md: 14px, lg: 24px, full: 9999px.
- Minimal elevation (`shadow-sm`), relying on surface contrast.
